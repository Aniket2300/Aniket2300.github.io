ABOUT

Grounded in fundamentals, curious about the frontier.

I'm an MCA student at Amity University, Kolkata, with a BCA background from Adamas University. My work sits at the intersection of classical ML — the kind you can explain with a precision/recall table — and the newer LLM/RAG tooling that's reshaping how software gets built.

I care about models that can defend their own decisions. Most of my projects pair a working classifier with an explainability layer, because a prediction without a reason isn't something I'd trust in production — or ask anyone else to.

Outside of coursework, I'm usually deep in a Jupyter notebook, debugging a Streamlit app, or documenting a project's failure modes as carefully as its wins.
